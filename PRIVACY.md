# Privacy Policy for Cleo - Custom New Tab

**Last Updated:** August 2026

Cleo is a privacy-first browser extension. All data processed by Cleo remains strictly on your local device.

## Data Usage & Storage
* **Local Preference Storage:** Cleo saves your dashboard preferences (such as custom colors, layout settings, font styles, and custom shortcut links) directly to your browser's local storage.
* **Top Sites (Web History):** Cleo requests the `topSites` permission solely to fetch and display your most frequently visited websites as speed-dial shortcuts on your new tab page when enabled. This data is read locally to render the user interface and is never transmitted off your device.
* **Favicons:** Cleo accesses website favicons locally to display icons for your shortcuts and search engine selector.

## Data Sharing & Transmission
Cleo does not collect, track, log, or share any personal information, browsing history, or usage analytics with external servers, third-party services, or the developer. The extension operates completely offline.
